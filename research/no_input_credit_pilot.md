# One-account no-input UAT pilot
Copy UPI and pay exact displayed amount, then I Have Paid submits no personal data. Merchant explicitly chooses discount budget when creating checkout (API defaults to zero). Payable amount is separate from invoice amount. Existing orders are preserved. The account row lock serializes allocations in PostgreSQL; unique constraints guard allocation; all historical unreserved legacy amounts are excluded. Pilot never reuses an amount, including expired orders. Exhaustion returns 409 rather than reassigning unsafe slots. Minimum payable ₹1. This is deliberately finite and not a production-scale recycling policy. Amount matching is diagnostic only: unrelated transfers and shared receiving bank accounts can collide. No live source, financial status change, or replay consumption is enabled. Existing n8n dispatch remains PENDING.

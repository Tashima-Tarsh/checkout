# Inline Agentic Vision research checkout
On a deployed, enabled lab, sign in as the merchant owning the checkout. Open /checkout/<order-id>?vision_lab=1. The panel is mounted only after a tenant-authorized capability response. Choose an authorized payment image and analyze. Successful OCR and matching amount produce "Visual payment success recognized. Research analysis complete." Unknown, negative, ambiguous or timed-out analysis cannot show that completion. The 20s client deadline bounds the request, not transaction freshness. Images are not stored; no financial state mutation, fulfillment, automatic capture or telemetry. The actual checkout status remains independent. Normal checkout is unchanged without the explicit lab flag. No real customer receipt committed.

The inline panel uses an accessible eye/scan icon rather than a visible heading. Consent and the distinction between image analysis and debit remain visible. Diagnostic controls are isolated on a separate page.

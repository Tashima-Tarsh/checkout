A growing business deserves a better way to manage its payments.

Behind every payment is a business working to serve customers.

India's digital payment journey has changed how businesses serve their customers, but the challenges of managing those payments have not disappeared. Businesses still need to understand their payment expenses, manage settlement arrangements, maintain transaction records and find solutions that fit their commercial requirements.

DISHA 8.2 was developed with these realities in mind. It brings together the software businesses need to manage supported payment activities while keeping the actual movement of money within the applicable banking and payment system.

Our purpose is to give businesses greater flexibility over their payment operations without DISHA 8.2 becoming another custodian of their funds.

Why DISHA 8.2 exists

Because accepting payments should not become an unnecessary burden on business

Every business works with its own margins, expenses and cash-flow requirements. A payment service charge that appears small on an individual transaction can become a significant annual expense when the business processes thousands of payments.

Beyond charges, businesses also need to account for settlement schedules, payment records and the time spent managing their commercial operations. These challenges become more noticeable as the business grows.

DISHA 8.2 was created to offer a different approach, one that focuses on the software surrounding payments rather than taking possession of the money being transferred.

A commercial relationship built around software, not the custody of your money

What makes DISHA 8.2 different

DISHA 8.2 provides a software service designed to help businesses manage their payment experience. The platform does not operate by collecting merchant funds into a DISHA 8.2 settlement account or retaining those funds before releasing them to the business.

This distinction allows us to focus on the needs of merchants, including payment management, transaction visibility and a more organised customer experience.

Commercial terms are discussed directly with each business, taking into consideration its requirements, operational scale and the services it needs.

A payment experience that keeps customers focused on their purchase

Customer experience

Customers expect a straightforward experience when they make a payment. They should be able to understand the amount, follow the available payment instructions and receive clear information about the transaction.

DISHA 8.2 focuses on keeping this process simple while providing businesses with the relevant information needed to manage their payment activities.

Zero fraud objective

Transaction confidence

Businesses depend on accurate payment information. An unclear transaction status can create uncertainty for the customer, delay fulfilment and increase the amount of work required to resolve a payment.

DISHA 8.2 is designed around a Zero Fraud objective, with an emphasis on responsible transaction checks and clearly communicated payment outcomes.

We believe that an unconfirmed payment should never be presented as a confirmed financial event.

Clear transaction information

Business receipts

A payment does not end when a customer completes the available payment steps. Businesses and customers also need dependable information about the transaction.

DISHA 8.2 aims to provide a clear record of the available payment information, including transaction references and receipts where the necessary confirmation has been established.

Use actual verified transaction fields. Never show a successful financial receipt solely because a browser callback or customer claim has been received.

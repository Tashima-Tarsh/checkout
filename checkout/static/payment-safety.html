Payment safety

Zero fraud objective

Businesses depend on accurate payment information. An unclear transaction status can create uncertainty for the customer, delay fulfilment and increase the amount of work required to resolve a payment.

DISHA 8.2 is designed around a Zero Fraud objective, with an emphasis on responsible transaction checks and clearly communicated payment outcomes.

Financially verified remains false until server-confirmed debit match. An unconfirmed payment should never be presented as a confirmed financial event.

No screenshot, UTR, browser resume or synthetic local signing may set financially_verified=true. All verification is server-side.

MCC (Merchant Category Code) is optional P2M metadata, not debit evidence. DISHA 8.2 does not rely on MCC for payment verification.

Transaction receipts include UTR/RRN and SHA-256 audit trail where available. Businesses receive clear transaction information for their records.

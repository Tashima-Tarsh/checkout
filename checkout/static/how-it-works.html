How DISHA 8.2 works

DISHA 8.2 is a payment software product designed to help businesses manage supported payment activities. The platform does not operate by collecting merchant funds into a DISHA 8.2 settlement account or retaining those funds before releasing them to the business.

A business creates a checkout link through the DISHA 8.2 software. The customer pays via their UPI app. The checkout link is unique and time-bound (120 seconds). Sharing the link does not confirm payment.

The customer pays from their UPI app. DISHA 8.2 observes the payment through supported payment channels. No screenshot, UTR entry, browser resume or synthetic local signing may set financially_verified=true.

DISHA 8.2 matches the debit through server-confirmed payment evidence. Financially verified remains false until server-confirmed debit match. This is the zero-fraud principle.
